How do I write a conversion?

Type it the way you would say it: 5 km, 72 °F to C, 100 usd in eur, 3pm Tokyo, 2 cups flour, tip 80 split 3, or a question like how many cm in 5 inches. Pick a target from the row above the keyboard. When the field is empty, “What can I convert?” lists everything.

What is Vandla Pro?

A one-time purchase, not a subscription, that unlocks travel mode, unlimited camera use and Ask anything (on iPhones with Apple Intelligence). Without it, the camera can be used three times a day; everything else is free. Buy it in Settings › Vandla Pro. It’s shared with your family through Family Sharing.

I bought Vandla Pro, but it isn’t unlocked

On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling Vandla, Pro comes back by itself when you’re signed in with the same Apple Account. If it doesn’t, open Settings › Vandla Pro › Restore Purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How does travel mode work?

Travel mode is part of Vandla Pro. Turn it on in Settings › Travel and choose a trip currency or destination. Plain numbers are then read as prices in the trip currency, the destination’s time is shown, you can save prices to the trip wallet, and the rate appears on the Lock Screen.

Why don’t I see converted prices in the camera?

Prices with a currency sign convert on their own. For menus without one, tap “Prices in …” at the top of the scanner and choose the currency.

“Ask anything” doesn’t answer

It needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri). Everything else in Vandla works without it.

How current are exchange rates?

Vandla uses the European Central Bank’s reference rates, published once per working day. The date of the rates is shown under each currency result. They keep working offline.
