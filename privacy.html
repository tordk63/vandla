Privacy Policy

Vandla · Last updated 31 October 2026

Vandla is built to work on your iPhone without collecting anything about you. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Everything you type, your history, your settings, travel mode and the expenses in your trip wallet are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

The camera is used only while the scanner is open, to read prices and text. Images are processed on your device and are not saved or sent anywhere.

Translation uses Apple’s on-device Translation framework, and “Ask anything” uses Apple Intelligence on your device. Neither sends your text to us.

Exchange rates

To show currency conversions, Vandla downloads daily exchange rates from the European Central Bank via the free service frankfurter.dev. These requests contain no personal information and nothing you typed. Like any internet request, they reveal your device’s IP address to that service, which states that it does not track users.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Children

Vandla collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
